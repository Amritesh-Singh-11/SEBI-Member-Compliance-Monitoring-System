## Initial Regulatory Scope: Stock Brokers

The primary market intermediary category modeled in this academic prototype is **STOCK BROKER**.

### Modeled Regulatory Requirements (Academic Representations)

1. **CYB-SB-01: Quarterly Cybersecurity Audit & Vulnerability Assessment Report**
   - **Category**: Cybersecurity
   - **Frequency**: Quarterly
   - **Severity**: HIGH
   - **Reference**: SEBI/HO/MIRSD/CIR/P/2018/147
   - **Academic Note**: Modeled to require document evidence upload (PDF/DOCX) confirming system audit completed by CERT-In empaneled auditor.

2. **FIN-SB-02: Monthly Net Worth & Capital Adequacy Certificate**
   - **Category**: Financial
   - **Frequency**: Monthly
   - **Severity**: CRITICAL
   - **Reference**: SEBI Circular MIRSD/SE/Cir-19/2009
   - **Academic Note**: Verifies minimum net worth threshold compliance for active trading operations.

3. **INV-SB-03: Half-Yearly Investor Grievance Redressal Report**
   - **Category**: Investor Protection
   - **Frequency**: Half-Yearly
   - **Severity**: MEDIUM
   - **Reference**: SEBI/HO/MIRSD/DOC/P/CIR/2021/42
   - **Academic Note**: Tracks pending vs resolved investor complaints filed against the stock broker.

4. **GOV-SB-04: Annual Compliance Officer Certificate & Governance Filing**
   - **Category**: Governance
   - **Frequency**: Annual
   - **Severity**: HIGH
   - **Reference**: SEBI (Stock Brokers) Regulations, 1992
   - **Academic Note**: Mandates declaration of adherence to SEBI Code of Conduct for Stock Brokers.

5. **OPS-SB-05: Annual System & Business Continuity Plan Audit**
   - **Category**: Operational
   - **Frequency**: Annual
   - **Severity**: HIGH
   - **Reference**: SEBI/HO/MIRSD/DOP/CIR/P/2019/75
   - **Academic Note**: Ensures broker DR/BCP testing has been conducted within the prescribed 12-month period.
